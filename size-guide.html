Size Guide

Sock sizing runs on foot length and calf comfort rather than shoe size alone. Use the chart below, then check the notes for each style family.

Find your size by foot length

Measure your foot from heel to longest toe while standing, in centimeters or inches, and match it to the ranges below. When you're between two sizes, we generally recommend sizing up for wool blends (they hold their shape better with slightly more room) and staying true to size for cotton crew and ankle styles.

Size US Shoe Size Foot Length
Small5 – 722 – 24 cm
Medium7.5 – 1024.5 – 27 cm
Large10.5 – 1327.5 – 29.5 cm

Notes by style family

Crew & Ankle (cotton blend): True to the chart above. The ribbed cuff has moderate stretch, so it stays snug without a strong compression feel.

Cloud Wool: Runs slightly roomier by design to allow for a thicker layer against cold. If you're between Medium and Large, choose Large for wool styles.

Novelty & Fun prints: Cut on the same block as our Classic Crew, so use your regular size from the chart above.

Still unsure?

Reach out through our contact page with your usual shoe size and brand, and we'll recommend a size before you order — no obligation either way.
