What we track, and how to manage it

Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website, used to remember information about your visit, like your preferences or how you arrived at the site. They're a standard part of how most modern websites function, and fuzzystocking.com uses them for a handful of specific, limited purposes described below. Cookies don't typically contain personally identifying information on their own, though they can be linked to information you've provided, such as when you're logged into an account. This policy explains what cookies we use and how you can manage them.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to keep our site functioning properly, such as remembering items in your cart as you browse between pages, and to understand general site usage patterns that help us improve the shopping experience. Some cookies are strictly necessary for core site functionality, while others support analytics and, occasionally, marketing efforts like remembering that you've already seen a particular promotional banner. We do not use cookies to build detailed personal profiles for sale to third parties. Our goal is to use the minimum set of cookies needed to run a functional, reasonably personalized site.

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential cookies are required for basic site functionality, such as maintaining your shopping cart contents as you move between pages, and cannot be disabled without affecting core site features. Analytics cookies, primarily through Google Analytics, help us understand which pages are visited most often, how long visitors spend on the site, and general traffic patterns, all in an aggregated, largely anonymized form. Preference cookies remember choices you've made, such as whether you've dismissed a particular notification, so you're not shown the same prompt repeatedly. We currently use a limited amount of marketing-related cookie functionality, and any expansion of that use will be reflected in an updated version of this policy.

Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on our site are placed by third-party services we use, most notably Google Analytics, which helps us understand site traffic and usage patterns. These third parties may collect information about your visit to our site and potentially other sites you visit, subject to their own privacy and cookie practices. We encourage you to review Google's privacy policy directly if you'd like more detail on how they handle data collected through Analytics. We select third-party providers that maintain reasonable data practices, but we don't control how they process data beyond what's outlined in our agreements with them.

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings, including blocking cookies entirely, deleting existing cookies, or being notified when a new cookie is set. Disabling essential cookies may affect basic site functionality, such as your shopping cart not retaining items as you browse. You can also opt out of Google Analytics tracking specifically using tools like the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, available directly from Google. We'd recommend reviewing your specific browser's help documentation for exact steps, since the process varies slightly between browsers.

Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect changes in the cookies we use or applicable legal requirements. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page, and for significant changes we may provide additional notice on the site. We encourage you to check this page occasionally if cookie usage is a particular concern for you. Continued use of our site after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
