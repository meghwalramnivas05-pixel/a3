Your information, handled plainly

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how FuzzyStocking ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit fuzzystocking.com or place an order with us. We know handing over your name, address, and email to a small sock brand requires some trust, and we've tried to write this policy in plain language rather than dense legal text. By using our site or placing an order, you agree to the practices described below. If anything here is unclear, our contact page is the fastest way to ask us directly.

Information We Collect

When you place an order, we collect the information needed to fulfill it: your name, shipping and billing address, email address, phone number, and payment details processed through our payment provider. When you sign up for our newsletter, we collect your email address and nothing more unless you provide it voluntarily. We also automatically collect some technical information when you browse our site, including your IP address, browser type, device type, and the pages you visit, gathered through standard analytics tools. We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information such as government identification numbers, and we ask that you avoid including anything like that in contact form messages.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide primarily to process and ship your order, respond to customer service questions, and send order-related updates like shipping confirmations. If you've opted into our newsletter, we use your email to send occasional product updates, restock alerts, and promotional offers, and you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send. We use aggregated, anonymized browsing data to understand which pages and products are popular, which helps us decide what to knit more of. We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we only share it with service providers, like our payment processor and shipping carriers, to the extent necessary to complete a transaction.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our site uses cookies and similar technologies, including Google Analytics, to understand how visitors use the site and to help us improve it over time. These tools may collect information such as pages visited, time spent on the site, and general location based on IP address, but they do not collect your name or payment details directly. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, though some site features may not work as intended if you do. For more detail on the specific cookies we use, see our separate Cookie Policy.

Third-Party Services

We rely on a small number of trusted third-party services to run our business, including a payment processor to handle transactions securely, a shipping carrier to deliver orders, and Google Analytics to understand site traffic. Each of these providers has its own privacy practices governing how they handle data, and we encourage you to review their policies if you'd like more detail. We choose providers that maintain reasonable security standards, but we cannot control or guarantee their practices beyond what's outlined in our agreements with them. We do not share more information with these providers than is necessary for them to perform their specific function.

Data Retention and Security

We retain order and account information for as long as necessary to fulfill orders, handle any returns or exchanges under our comfort guarantee, and meet basic bookkeeping and tax obligations. We use reasonable technical and administrative safeguards to protect your information, including secure payment processing that never stores full card details on our own servers. That said, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security against every possible risk. If you have reason to believe your account or order information has been compromised, please contact us right away.

Your Rights and Choices

You can request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct inaccurate information, or request that we delete your data, subject to any legal or operational obligations that may require us to retain certain records. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time, and you can disable cookies through your browser as described above. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy laws, and we'll do our best to honor reasonable requests consistent with those laws. To exercise any of these rights, reach out through our contact page and we'll respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Children's Privacy

Our site and products are intended for general audiences, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information without appropriate consent, please contact us so we can review and remove it as needed. We do not knowingly market directly to children, and our newsletter sign-up and checkout process are designed with an adult user in mind. If we become aware that we've inadvertently collected information from a child, we will take reasonable steps to delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. When we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page, and for significant changes we may provide additional notice, such as a banner on our site or an email to newsletter subscribers. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we handle your information. Continued use of our site after a change becomes effective constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests related to this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, please reach out through our contact page, by phone at +1-888-777-5845, or by mail at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States. We aim to respond to privacy-related inquiries within a reasonable timeframe, generally within a few business days. We take these questions seriously, even when the answer is a simple one, and we'd rather you ask than wonder.
