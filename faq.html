Frequently Asked Questions & Advisory

Atelier Sourcing & Material Integrity

All provisions and materials are sourced under direct single-estate contracts with certified landrace growers and artisanal mills, preserving certified un-hybridized heirloom genetics.

Every harvest batch undergoes rigorous liquid chromatography and mass spectrometry testing at our 181 Mercer Street laboratory to ensure 0% contaminants and peak active compounds.

Bespoke Orders & Private Dining

Due to intimate seating capacities in our SoHo Manhattan tasting room, we recommend booking private salon reservations 14 to 21 days in advance via our concierge desk (+1-888-777-5845).

Yes. Our master culinary team designs individualized multi-course degustations accommodating gluten-free, vegan, diabetic, and allergen-sensitive parameters upon advance notice.

Shipping, Cellar Packaging & Logistics

All dry provisions are sealed in UV-protective, nitrogen-flushed earthenware crocks and lined thermal canisters that protect sensitive enzymes from oxidation during transit.

Domestic consignments within the United States arrive in 2 to 4 business days via insured express couriers. International air freight consignments arrive in 5 to 8 business days.

Preservation, Storage & Lifespan

Store in opaque ceramic or stoneware vessels at 16°C to 20°C in a dark, dry pantry away from direct sunlight to preserve cellular integrity and delicate natural oils.
